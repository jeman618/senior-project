# Project Vision:

It is an application that provides the user information about fruits and vegetables and how to grow them. A user can search for a plant and read general information about the plants, such as what they are, what they are gardened for, and how to garden them. The instructions will vary since not all plants are the same. I’m thinking of including the more popular plants to grow such as strawberries, apples, tomatoes, cabbage, avocado, squash, potatoes, and more.

The user can also sign in to be able to favorite certain plants so that they do not have to search it every time, receive notifications to be reminded to take care of their plants and what they specifically need, and can also share their location (maybe zip code or GPS location) to see which plants are best to grow for the location provided, which opens some avenues for mapping and gathering weather data. Other features may appear later.

The reasoning behind this idea is that I have spent considerable time in the agriculture sector, and I figured I can mold that with my CS knowledge.
